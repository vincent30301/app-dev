# My Favorite Movie: Green Book

## Introduction
My favorite movie is **Green Book**, a 2018 comedy-drama film directed by Peter Farrelly.

I like this movie because it shows the importance of friendship, respect, and understanding despite differences between people.

## Movie Information
- **Title:** Green Book
- **Release Year:** 2018
- **Genre:** Comedy-Drama
- **Director:** Peter Farrelly
- **Main Actors:** Viggo Mortensen and Mahershala Ali

## About the Story
The movie follows **Tony Lip**, an Italian-American driver, who works for **Dr. Don Shirley**, a talented African-American pianist. They travel together through the southern United States during the 1960s.

During their journey, they experience different challenges and slowly develop a strong friendship.

## Why I Like This Movie
I like *Green Book* because it teaches us that:
1. Friendship can develop between people with different backgrounds.
2. We should respect and understand other people.
3. Kindness and acceptance are important in our lives.

> "The movie reminds us that everyone deserves respect and kindness."

## My Favorite Part
My favorite part is seeing how Tony and Dr. Shirley slowly understand each other and become good friends despite their differences.
